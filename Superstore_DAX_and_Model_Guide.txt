MODEL RELATIONSHIPS (single direction, dimension -> fact):
Dim Date[Date] (1) -> Fact Orders[Order Date] (*)
Dim Customer[Customer ID] (1) -> Fact Orders[Customer ID] (*)
Dim Product[Product ID] (1) -> Fact Orders[Product ID] (*)

Use Dim Date[Date] as the date slicer. Mark Dim Date as the date table using Dim Date[Date].
Sort Dim Date[Month] by Dim Date[Month Number] and Dim Date[Year-Month] by Dim Date[Year-Month Sort].
If you add a second date relationship for Ship Date, keep it inactive unless you explicitly need shipping-date analysis.

MEASURES (create with Modeling > New measure):
Total Sales = SUM('Fact Orders'[Sales])

Total Profit = SUM('Fact Orders'[Profit])

Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT('Fact Orders'[Order ID])

Total Quantity = SUM('Fact Orders'[Quantity])

Profit Margin % = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)

Sales Previous Year =
CALCULATE([Total Sales], DATEADD('Dim Date'[Date], -1, YEAR))

YoY Sales Growth % =
DIVIDE([Total Sales] - [Sales Previous Year], [Sales Previous Year], 0)

Average Discount = AVERAGE('Fact Orders'[Discount])
